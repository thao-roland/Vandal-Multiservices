About

A small atelier.
A long memory.

Vandal was founded on a simple idea: that the quiet, repetitive work of caring for a home deserves the same rigour as any fine craft. Twelve years on, the team is still small, the standards still uncompromised.

Philosophy

We believe a finished room should feel inevitable — as if it had always been that way.

This conviction shapes everything: the materials we source, the order in which we work, the silence we keep on a job site. We do not rush, we do not improvise, and we do not leave until the floor is swept clean of our presence.

It is, perhaps, an old-fashioned way of working. We prefer it that way.
